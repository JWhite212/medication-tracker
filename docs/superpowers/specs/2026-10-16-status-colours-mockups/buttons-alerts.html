The app's own dark theme and default accent. Buttons get fixed heights, so compact density can no longer make a "large" button shorter than a "small" one. Dose controls are not on this page and stay exactly as they are.

Button — four kinds, two sizes

Kindmd · 44pxsm · 36pxUsed for Primary The one main action on a form or page. Accent fill. Secondary Everything else, including reversible "turn it off" actions: Archive, Sign out, Disable 2FA, Adjust stock, Revoke. Neutral, strong border. Ghost Low-emphasis: Cancel, Back, Clear. Danger Only actions that can't be undone, and only inside the confirm step. Solid red. Never used for reversible actions.
